# Invoice Discrepancy Investigation

An invoice discrepancy exists when the recorded invoice amount differs from the amount reconstructed using authoritative usage data and applicable pricing rules.

The investigation should compare the recorded amount, reconstructed amount, relevant pricing version, billable usage, and supporting invoice evidence.

A difference between expected and recorded amounts confirms a discrepancy but does not independently establish its root cause.

Financial corrections require appropriate review and authorization. The investigation agent must not modify invoices, customer plans, or prices.
