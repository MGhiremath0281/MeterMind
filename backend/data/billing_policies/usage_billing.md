# Usage-Based Billing and Pricing

Usage-based charges must be calculated using the customer's applicable plan and the pricing version effective during the billing period.

The effective date of a pricing version determines when that version may be applied. A later pricing version must not automatically be applied retroactively.

Expected usage charges are calculated from eligible billable usage multiplied by the applicable unit price, according to the plan's billing rules.

The deterministic billing engine is the authoritative source for reconstructed invoice amounts. A policy document provides context but does not replace the calculation.
